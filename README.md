# Hi, welcome to VehLinker! 🚗🔗

![VehLinker banner](./VehLinker_intro.png)

> -- *Link Every Vehicle*. （连接每一辆车）
> The banner illustration was drawn as SVG and rendered with Claude.

VehLinker is an organization that builds software for connecting vehicles: the cars on the road, the data they produce, and the people and systems that rely on them. Our goal is to make that link simple, reliable and easy to build on, so you can focus on your product instead of the plumbing.

We are open source friendly and open to any feedback. If you have an idea, a question or a bug report, we look forward to hearing from you.

## Build the future of connected vehicles together

* Browse our [repositories](https://github.com/orgs/VehLinker/repositories) and star the ones you like.
* Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue in the relevant repository.
* Want to contribute? Fork a project, make your change and send a pull request. Every contribution is welcome.
* Follow [@VehLinker](https://github.com/VehLinker) to stay up to date with new releases.
